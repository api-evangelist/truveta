By Hannah Burkhardt, PhD , Principal Software Engineer at Truveta As a member of the biomedical informatics research community, I was incredibly excited to join Truveta. From the beginning, it was striking to see the company solving—at national scale—problems that researchers in my field have been working on for decades. Since joining, one of my personal goals has been to help bring greater visibility to Truveta’s technical innovations within the broader informatics community, which spans academia, industry, and government.
